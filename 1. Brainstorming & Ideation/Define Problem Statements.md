# Define Problem Statements

**Project:** EduGenie – Your Smart Budget & Recommendation Assistant  
**Team ID:** SWTID-2026-5933

## Customer Problem Statement Template

| Problem Statement (PS) | I am (Customer) | I'm trying to | But | Because | Which makes me feel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PS-1 | A student or young earner | Track where my money goes | I forget or skip recording expenses | Manual tracking is tiring and time-consuming | Out of control |
| PS-2 | A person with a monthly income | Stay within a monthly budget | I overspend before month-end | I get no warning when a category limit is near | Stressed and worried |
| PS-3 | Someone who wants to save money | Find practical ways to save | I do not know which expenses to cut | Generic tips do not match my spending habits | Confused and discouraged |
| PS-4 | A person planning purchases | Decide whether I can afford something | I cannot judge the effect on my budget | I do not have a forecast of my future spending | Uncertain and anxious |

## Problem Statement Summary

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Core Problem | People lack a simple, personalised tool to track, plan and improve their spending |
| Who Is Affected | Students, young professionals and families managing monthly budgets |
| Proposed Direction | An AI assistant that tracks expenses, plans budgets, forecasts spending and recommends ways to save |
